# TrueBinding

> TrueBinding, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotherapeutic company in Santa Clara, California, founded
> in 2016. It develops humanized monoclonal antibodies against Galectin-3 - led by TB006, in
> clinical development for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease - and sells cGMP contract
> development and manufacturing (CDMO) services from its own Santa Clara facility.
